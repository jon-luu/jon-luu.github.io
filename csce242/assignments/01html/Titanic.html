The Story of the Titanic

The RMS Titanic was a British passenger ship that set sail from Southampton, England on April 10, 1912. It was on the way to New York City before it struck an iceberg. It was the largest ship ever built during its time, spanning 882 feet long. It was consdiered the safest ship of its time. The ship carried over 2,200 passengers and crew, ranging from wealthy businessmen in first class to people seeking a new life in America. Just four days into the voyage, on April 15 at 2:20 AM, the Titanic struck an iceberg in the North Atlantic and began sinking.

Within about two and a half hours, the Titanic sank in the freezing waters where than 1,500 people lost their lives. It remains one of the deadliest maritime disasters in history. The ship did not carry enough lifeboats for everyone on board which led to major changes in maritime safety regulations. Survivors were eventually picked up by the RMS Carpathia, which arrived a few hours after the sinking and carried them to New York.

Random Facts About the Titanic

  • There were 12 dogs on board and there was supposed to be a dog show the morning of the sinking
  • The fourth smokestack was built just for looks
  • Violet Jessop, a ship nurse, survived major crashes on all three sister ships: the Olympic, the Titanic, and the Britannic.
