Important Dates for Legos

Year Event Why It Mattered
1932 Company founded by Ole Kirk Christiansen Started as a wooden toy workshop in Billund
1934 Company named "Lego" Short for the Danish "leg godt" (play well)
1947 Bought a plastic injection molding machine Opened the door to plastic brick production
1958 Stud-and-tube brick design patented Created the interlocking system still used today
1978 Minifigure introduced Gave Lego sets a character to build stories around
