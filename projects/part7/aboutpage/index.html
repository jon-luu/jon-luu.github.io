Cola Clothes

A secondhand outdoor store for Columbia hikers, campers, and climbers. We connect local adventurers with pre-loved gear, trail-tested stories, and a community that keeps the outdoors moving.

Gear That Keeps the Trail Alive

Cola Clothes started as a local gear swap between friends in Columbia. We realized that the best outdoor gear was often retired too early - still waterproof, still warm, still ready for the next ridge. So we built a secondhand store that helps hikers, campers, and climbers buy and sell gear with people who understand the value of a well-loved shell, a reliable stove, and a pack that has already seen the sunrise.

Today, our store is a gathering place for the local outdoor community. We host gear swaps, repair workshops, and trail meetups that keep the spirit of adventure moving. Whether you are outfitting a first backpacking trip or upgrading for a bigger objective, every purchase here supports a more circular, more local way to enjoy the outdoors.

Why We Reuse

The values that keep our gear, our trails, and our community moving.

Circular Gear

We believe the best gear should be used until it is truly worn out, not just replaced. By keeping high-quality equipment in circulation, we reduce waste, lower the cost of entry for new adventurers, and preserve the stories that make every piece special.

Local Community

We are built for the Columbia outdoor scene. Our store connects buyers and sellers who know the same trails, the same weather, and the same gear needs. That makes every transaction feel more personal, more trustworthy, and more rooted in the community.

Trail-Tested Quality

We look for gear that has been cared for, cleaned, and used responsibly. Every listing is reviewed for condition, authenticity, and the kind of detail that matters when you are planning a long hike, a cold camp, or a rainy summit push.

Accessible Adventure

Secondhand gear makes the outdoors more approachable. We help new adventurers build a reliable kit without the full-retail price, and we help experienced explorers upgrade, downsize, or refresh their setup without breaking the bank.
